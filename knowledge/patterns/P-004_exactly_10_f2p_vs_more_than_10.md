# P-004: Exactly 10 f2p ≠ live “more than 10”

**Status:** approved  
**Reusable:** yes

## Pattern

Exactly 10 meaningful fail-to-pass tests meets the official guideline minimum. Live confirmation of “More than 10” requires ≥11. Never claim more than 10 when count is 10.

## What to do

Add one meaningful f2p before selecting the live “more than 10” checkbox, or do not select it.
